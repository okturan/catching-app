# Landing page (app/views/pages/home.html.erb)

Scope: the marketing landing page at `/`, Persuade mode. It sets the visual world every app surface inherits (layout, planning form, participation page, dashboard, Devise forms, static pages), which are Operate surfaces built in the same world under this contract.

Audience: a friend who has been asked to organize something across time zones, arriving from a chat or a search, often on a phone. Job: understand in seconds that this finds a time that works for everyone in everyone's own zone with nobody signing up, then press Plan a meeting. Proof: live synchronized clocks in real cities and an authored, labelled sample grid; no testimonials, numbers or logos exist and none may be invented. Binding: the name, catching.app, the tagline "We have some catching app to do!" and its playful tone. Dropped: the 2021 carousel videos and every Cloudinary URL.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One instant read five ways. A row of synchronized wall clocks under enamel city plates is the offer and the proof; refuses the headline-plus-app-screenshot scheduler page.

OWN-WORLD: warm painted wall #F2EFE9 as ground; white glass clock faces with black hands and a red sweeping second hand #D7262A; enamel-blue plates #24405E with white engraved caps for labels and primary actions; Schibsted Grotesk for words, Martian Mono for every time; no gradients, no glass blur, no imagery hosted elsewhere.

STORY: the visitor sees the same second ticking in five cities, drags the shared moment and watches every clock follow, understands "in everyone's own zone, nobody signs up", and presses Plan a meeting.

FIRST VIEWPORT: full-width row of five live clocks (visitor's zone first), each above its enamel city plate reading city and local time; beneath, the tagline as the headline at display scale, one sentence of offer, the Plan a meeting plate button and a quiet "Lost your organizer link?" text link. Signature interaction: a draggable time ruler under the clocks that moves all five together and shows the hours everyone is awake in yellow #FFC65A.

FORM: Newsroom Clock Wall, candidate 1 of my ordered list (IMPECCABLE'S PICK), chosen by the user over the assigned World-Time Dial; seed key 080a80c0.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
